Product Analytic Account for Odoo

Effortlessly manage analytic distributions for products across invoices, sales, and purchases

Simplify Analytic Distribution

Set default analytic distributions on products and auto-apply them

The product_analytic_account module adds an "Analytic Distribution" field to product templates, allowing you to define default analytic distributions. These are automatically applied to invoice lines, sales order lines, and purchase order lines when the product is selected, streamlining your accounting processes.

Setting analytic distribution in the product form

Key Features

  • Add an "Analytic Distribution" field to product templates for easy configuration.
  • Automatically populate analytic distributions in invoice lines when selecting a product.
  • Apply default analytic distributions to sales order lines and purchase order lines.
  • Support for customizable analytic precision to control decimal places.
Demonstrating automatic analytic distribution in purchase orders

Usage Steps

  1. Configure a Product: Open the product form and set the desired analytic distribution (e.g., 50% to Azad ZS-L007, 50% to Grading Department).
    2. Setting analytic distribution in the product form
  2. Create a Purchase Order: Add the product to a purchase order line, and the analytic distribution will sync automatically.
    3. Analytic distribution auto-applied in a purchase order
  3. Create a Sales Order: Select the product in a sales order, and the analytic distribution will be applied.
    4. Analytic distribution auto-applied in a sales order
  4. Create an Invoice: Add the product to an invoice line, and the analytic distribution will auto-populate.
    5. Analytic distribution auto-applied in an invoice

Requirements

This module depends on the following Odoo modules:
product, account, sale, purchase, analytic
